# Contribution Guidelines

Thank you for suggesting something. Please make sure your pull request follows these rules:

- One entry per pull request, with a short reason in the description of why it belongs.
- The entry is about how work with agents is organised: planning, tasks, prompts and skills, review, approval, or running agents side by side. A general-purpose agent belongs in another list.
- The project is maintained, documented, and has been used by someone other than its author.
- Use the form `- [Name](link) - Description.` The description starts with a capital letter, ends with a full stop, and says what the thing does without marketing words.
- Add it at the end of its section, unless the section is clearly ordered otherwise.
- Say so if you maintain or are paid by the project you add.
- Check your spelling, and that the link works.
- The list is checked with [awesome-lint](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome-lint) on every pull request.

By contributing you agree that your contribution is released under [CC0](LICENSE).
